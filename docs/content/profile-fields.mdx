These fields belong inside **one entry of `profiles`** in your Helm values.
They describe the sandbox's starting image, commands, helpers and permitted
resource choices. See [Profiles](profiles.md) for how profiles work.

Select a field to inspect its purpose, ownership, editability and source
constraints. The native `agentSandbox.spec` and `openShell.spec` entries include
their portal restrictions and full examples. Reserved fields are explicitly
marked when unavailable.
